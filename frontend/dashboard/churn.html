Voter Registration Churn

Texas registration closed on October 5, 2026, and federal law bars systematic voter removals in the 90 days before the election — so the county rolls should barely move between now and November 3. This page tracks every county's registered-voter total against the Secretary of State's certified October 5 figures and flags any change, up or down.

How to read this

  • Δ since Oct 5 is the current total minus the Secretary of State's certified November 2026 figure. A drop during this window is the item worth attention; a rise is unusual after the deadline too. A dash means unchanged.
  • Suspense voters are registrations flagged for an address check. They can still vote, but a county with a large suspense share is where list-maintenance actions land hardest — useful context, shown as of October 5. (The live feed doesn't report suspense, so this column is the baseline only.)
  • This is net, not gross. We see each county's total, not individual additions and removals, so equal churn in both directions would look like no change. The full registration file isn't public; this is the honest limit of a free, aggregate source.
  • A change is a question, not a verdict. There are lawful reasons a roll shifts — deaths, felony convictions, confirmed moves, de-duplication, deadline processing. A flag here means "worth a look," nothing more.
