How to read this

Early vote is in-person plus mail ballots counted so far for the November 3, 2026 general election, per the Secretary of State's Civix feed. Turnout is that total as a share of the county's registered voters. These are the state's figures for every county.

Reconciled marks the counties where we also hold the county's own voter roster, so The Reconciler can match the two counts voter by voter and show where they agree or differ. Those are the eight on the main dashboard; the rest show the state's count only, until a county roster parser is added for them.
