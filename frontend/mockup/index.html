    06 · Context

    Why two sets of numbers

    What TEAM is

    TEAM is the state's voter registration database. Counties upload early-vote rosters to it, and the SOS "Early Voting Report" (served by the Civix EVR app) is built from those uploads. The county's own site is built from its e-pollbooks and mail-ballot logs directly, so it is usually a day ahead.

    A gap that closes within a day or two is normal lag. A gap that persists, or a past day that keeps changing, is what this page exists to surface.

    What each side counts

    How we collect

    • SOS: getFile?type=EVR_EARLYVOTING&electionId=53815 for every EV date, every 15 minutes. Each response is hashed; a changed hash is a new version. Nothing is overwritten.
    • Counties: PDFs, XLSX and HTML pages from the county sites, same cadence. Harris, Bexar and Hidalgo overwrite their files in place, so a missed capture is gone.
    • Public API serves aggregates only. Voter rosters are compared on VUID offline and never published.
